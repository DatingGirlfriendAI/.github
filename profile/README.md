## What the evening usually looks like

You open the site. You choose a ready character or build one with a name, a look and a short background. You send a message. The reply comes back in the same chat. If you come back the next day, the thread is still there. Earlier details are more likely to return on paid plans. Free tiers forget sooner.

Most of these tools run in the browser. Some have apps. Almost all ask you to confirm age. Images of the character and voice replies usually unlock on paid plans. The free start is short on purpose. It is a test of tone, not a full evening of unlimited chat.

---

## How people actually use it

1. Open the site and pick one character.  
2. Send a few messages and see if the tone fits the evening you want.  
3. Stay in that thread if you want continuity.  
4. Upgrade later if you want more messages, pictures or voice.

Most people try one ready character first. If the replies feel right, they stay or build another. If not, they close the tab. The free tier is usually enough to decide.

## What to check before you pay

- How many free messages you get. A handful is not a full evening.  
- What the paid plan actually adds.  
- Whether a promo code is active and what it covers.  
- Whether you can cancel from the account page.  
- Do not put real names, phone numbers or other personal details in the chat.

A couple of evenings of free use usually shows if the character holds the conversation. After that the choice is about media and how long you want to stay in one thread.

## Who it fits

- You want a character you can open when the evening is quiet.  
- You prefer one thread you can return to.  
- You are fine testing free before paying.  
- You may want images or voice later.

It is less useful if you only need a general chatbot with no character. The product is built around companions.
